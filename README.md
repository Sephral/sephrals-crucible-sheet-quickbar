Inspired by the Tidy5e sheet with its dense lists, play and edit mode, search and inline details, but in Crucible's own look. For heroes and adversaries alike.

## Highlights

* **Everything in view:** health, wounds, morale, defenses, action, focus and heroism always sit in the header. No more switching tabs in the middle of a turn.
* **The quickbar:** double click the title bar and the sheet becomes a small, translucent bar that stays open on the map. Favorites, spells and pinned skills, one click each.
* **The right action, fast:** searchable action lists with cost chips and a *usable now* filter that hides what you cannot pay right now. Hover an action to see which pips it costs.
* **A spellbook for composed spells:** save your favorite combinations of rune, gesture and inflection and cast them again with one click.
* **Made for combat:** your quickbar opens when your turn begins and tells you when you are next. Targeted tokens show their defenses, distance and conditions, and actions that cannot reach them are marked.
* **A combat bar for the GM:** on an adversary's turn its quickbar opens in a fixed spot and moves on to the next adversary.
* **Your sheet, your choice:** every user decides which characters open in it. One player can use it without changing anything for the others or the GM.
* **Your look:** dark and light theme, transparent, solid, Crucible or Ember codex backgrounds and a compact mode.

## How it works

1. Enable the module in your Crucible world. Nothing changes for anyone yet.
2. Right click your character in the actor directory and choose **Use Sheet & Quickbar (just for me)**.
3. Double click the title bar to switch between the full sheet and the quickbar.

The GM can also make it the default for everyone.
